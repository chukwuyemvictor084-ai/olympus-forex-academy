FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

A quick guide to Olympus training, class options and the enrollment process.

Who is the training for?

Olympus training is designed for beginners, developing traders and learners who want a more structured approach to forex education.

Can I take the classes online?

Yes. Select an online package in the application form. You can also request a physical class and Olympus will confirm availability and pricing.

Do I need previous forex experience?

No. The curriculum starts with fundamentals and progresses into technical analysis, risk management and advanced concepts.

How does the WhatsApp application work?

After you complete the form, your application details are formatted into a WhatsApp message addressed to Olympus at 09041004544 so you can continue the enrollment conversation.

Does the academy guarantee trading profits?

No. Forex trading involves substantial risk and results vary. The academy provides education and training, not guaranteed returns or personalized financial advice.

Are the online package prices monthly?

Yes. The Starter, Premium and Elite online packages are monthly plans, billed per month. Physical-class pricing is confirmed separately.

How do I choose a schedule?

Select your preferred schedule in the application. Olympus can then confirm the available class timing and next steps with you.
